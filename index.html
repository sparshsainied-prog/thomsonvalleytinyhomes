Thomson Valley Tiny Homes

Four tiny homes and a three-bedroom house on one block in Rawson, with Mt St Gwinear behind the town. Dogs welcome. Walhalla is 15 to 20 minutes away.

3 Stander Drive, RawsonUnits 1 to 4 and the Main House, a short stroll from Craters Lake.

Five places to stay, one gate

Each tiny home has its own bathroom, kitchenette and covered front deck. The Main House sleeps six, with a pool table, a pizza oven and a fenced backyard. Everyone shares the laundry and the fire pit.

Terry and Maxine run it. Dogs are welcome: tell them how many are coming when you book.
